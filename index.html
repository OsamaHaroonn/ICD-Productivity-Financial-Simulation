Finance & ROI (DCF)

▼
Project Lifetime (Years)
DCF horizon for NPV, IRR, and cash flow table
Ramp-Up Period (Years)
Years to reach full utilization. Yr 1 = 1/N of target util, rising linearly. Affects IRR, NPV, payback significantly.
Discount Rate / WACC (%)
Required rate of return / cost of capital. Shariff rule of thumb: IRR must exceed 15%.
Utilization Rate (%)
% of annual capacity actually used. Conservative = 80%.
OPEX Structure
Fixed OPEX (% of Civil+Eqpt/yr)
Calculated on civil + machinery only (NOT land or financing). Port rule-of-thumb: 3–6%.
Variable OPEX (RM/TEU)
Linked to actual handled TEUs (capacity × utilization). Default RM 80/TEU.
Financing Cost (%)
Adam: 0–10%, default 5%. Applied to (Construction + Soft Costs). Added to CAPEX, excluded from OPEX base.
